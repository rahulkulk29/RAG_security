# Published Commercial Litigation Case Studies & Defense Precedents

## Representative Public Case Summaries

### 1. *Apex Dynamics v. Vector Systems* (Delaware Court of Chancery)
LegalShield LLC defended Vector Systems against expedited preliminary injunction motions asserting trade secret misappropriation under the Delaware Uniform Trade Secrets Act. The Court denied the injunction in full, finding the asserted algorithms constituted standard public-domain industry knowledge.

### 2. *In re CloudScale Infrastructure Securities Litigation* (S.D.N.Y.)
Lead defense counsel for CloudScale's audit committee in a putative class action alleging revenue misstatements. Successfully secured a complete dismissal with prejudice under Rule 12(b)(6) for failure to plead scienter under the PSLRA.

### 3. *Nordic Telecom Cross-Border Arbitration* (ICC Zurich)
Represented international software vendor in multi-million dollar contract rescission arbitration, securing an arbitral award upholding the limitation of liability clauses and awarding full attorney fee recovery.
