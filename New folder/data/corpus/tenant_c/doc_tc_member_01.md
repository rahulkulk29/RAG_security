# Standard Corporate Mutual NDA & Restrictive Covenant Templates

## Internal Practice Guidance for Legal Associates
This document contains LegalShield LLC's standard approved bilateral Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) framework and negotiation fallbacks for corporate transactions.

## Standard Form NDA Terms (Version 2026.1)
1. **Definition of Confidential Information**: Encompasses proprietary technical data, trade secrets, software code, financial valuations, and customer rosters disclosed in writing or marked as proprietary.
2. **Standard Term of Non-Disclosure**: Standard 3-year survival period from disclosure date; trade secrets survive indefinitely so long as they remain protected under applicable law.
3. **Approved Fallback Provisions**:
   - If opposing counsel rejects 3-year term: Fallback to 24 months with senior associate sign-off.
   - If recipient demands carve-out for residual knowledge: Permit standard unaided-memory residual clause strictly excluding trade secrets and patents.
4. **Governing Law & Forum**: State of Delaware, Court of Chancery.
