# Litigation Practice Areas & Senior Counsel Directory

## Core Practice Groups
LegalShield LLC delivers specialized legal advocacy across four primary discipline groups:
1. **Commercial & Cross-Border Litigation**: Breach of contract, fiduciary duty disputes, post-closing M&A indemnification, and international arbitration.
2. **Intellectual Property & Trade Secret Defense**: Patent infringement litigation before federal district courts, ITC Section 337 investigations, and Defend Trade Secrets Act (DTSA) injunctions.
3. **Data Privacy & Regulatory Enforcement**: Defense against FTC consent orders, state attorney general investigations, and GDPR cross-border transfer challenges.
4. **Corporate Governance & Shareholder Actions**: Board advisory, special committee investigations, and derivative lawsuit defense.
