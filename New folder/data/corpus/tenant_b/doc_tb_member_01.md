# Project Pulse Clinical Triage Protocol & Urgent Escalation Guide

## Internal Clinical Operations Guidelines
This standard operating procedure governs clinical triage workflows within HealthTech's Project Pulse virtual care center. It applies to all registered nurses, triage coordinators, and supervising physicians.

## Triage Acuity Classification (Emergency Severity Index Adaptation)
- **Level 1 (Immediate Life Threat)**: Severe respiratory distress, active chest pain with diaphoresis, acute stroke symptoms (FAST positive).
  - *Action*: Initiate immediate 911 dispatch to patient physical location via Emergency Services Interface; keep patient on emergency bridge line.
- **Level 2 (Emergent / High Risk)**: Hypertensive urgency (SBP > 180 or DBP > 110 without acute end-organ damage), diabetic ketoacidosis symptoms, high fever in immunocompromised patient.
  - *Action*: Route to On-Call Attending Physician within 10 minutes.
- **Level 3 (Urgent)**: Uncomplicated UTI symptoms, mild asthma exacerbation responding to bronchodilators, localized cellulitis.
  - *Action*: Schedule virtual consultation within 2 hours.
- **Level 4 & 5 (Routine / Chronic)**: Prescription refills, routine follow-up reviews, mild rash.
  - *Action*: Queue for assigned care team asynchronous review within 24 hours.
