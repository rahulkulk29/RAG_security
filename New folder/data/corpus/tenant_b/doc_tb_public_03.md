# HealthTech Privacy Pledge & Notice of Patient Privacy Practices

## Commitment to Patient Confidentiality
HealthTech Inc complies with the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act of 1996 (HIPAA), HITECH Act amendments, and applicable state medical confidentiality statutes.

## Protected Health Information (PHI) Handling
1. **Uses and Disclosures for Treatment**: Your clinical care team accesses medical history, lab results, and medication records exclusively to coordinate medical care, diagnosis, and prescription management.
2. **Third-Party Disclosures**: HealthTech never sells patient data to commercial advertisers or unassociated third parties. Any partner labs or imaging centers operate under strict Business Associate Agreements (BAAs).
3. **Patient Rights**: Under HIPAA, patients possess the statutory right to inspect, copy, and request amendment of their designated medical record set, as well as obtain an accounting of historical disclosures.

For privacy complaints or inquiries, contact our Privacy Officer at `privacy@healthtech.internal` or call 1-800-555-0199.
