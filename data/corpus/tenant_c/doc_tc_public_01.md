# LegalShield LLC Terms of Engagement & Retainer Schedule

## About LegalShield LLC
LegalShield LLC is a specialized commercial litigation, corporate governance, and intellectual property defense firm representing mid-market and enterprise technology companies.

## Standard Client Engagement Terms
1. **Scope of Representation**: Legal representation commences upon mutual execution of our formal Engagement Agreement and receipt of the agreed initial retainer deposit.
2. **Billing Rates & Invoicing**:
   - Senior Partners: \$750 - \$950 / hour.
   - Special Counsel & Senior Associates: \$500 - \$650 / hour.
   - Paralegals & Technical E-Discovery Specialists: \$225 - \$300 / hour.
   - Detailed billing statements are rendered monthly with 30-day settlement terms.
3. **Escrow Trust Accounting**: All retainer deposits are maintained in segregated Interest on Lawyers' Trust Accounts (IOLTA) in accordance with state bar association regulations.

Initial consultations for potential commercial litigation matters may be requested through `inquiries@legalshield.internal`.
