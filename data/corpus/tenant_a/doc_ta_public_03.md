# FinTech Corp Security & Compliance Overview

## Regulatory Certifications
FinTech Corp maintains rigorous compliance certifications to ensure merchant trust and data safety:
- **PCI DSS Level 1**: Certified Service Provider under PCI DSS v4.0 with annual third-party Attestation of Compliance (AOC).
- **SOC 1 & SOC 2 Type II**: Audited by an independent CPA firm covering Security, Confidentiality, and Processing Integrity.
- **ISO/IEC 27001:2022**: Information Security Management System certification covering all payment processing infrastructure.
- **GDPR & CCPA**: Fully compliant privacy program providing data residency options in Dublin (EU-West-1) and Frankfurt (EU-Central-1).

## Network Security & Encryption Standards
- All data in transit requires TLS 1.3 encryption with Perfect Forward Secrecy (PFS).
- Cardholder data is tokenized immediately at ingest using HSM-backed AES-256-GCM encryption before entering primary storage.
- External penetration testing is performed quarterly by accredited third-party security assessors.
