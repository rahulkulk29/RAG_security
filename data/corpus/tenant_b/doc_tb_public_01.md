# HealthTech Patient Portal FAQ & Telehealth Services

## About HealthTech Inc
HealthTech Inc provides connected digital healthcare, remote patient monitoring (RPM), and secure virtual telehealth consultations for participating health networks across North America.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### How do I schedule a virtual telehealth consultation?
Patients can book appointments 24/7 through the HealthTech Patient Portal web application or mobile app. Video visits are conducted through WebRTC encrypted streams without requiring third-party plugins.

### How do I request prescription refills?
Select "Medications" from the main navigation panel, choose the active prescription, and submit a refill request directly to your assigned primary care provider. Approvals are typically transmitted to your preferred retail pharmacy within 24 business hours.

### Can I view my lab and diagnostic imaging results?
Routine blood panels and laboratory tests are published to the portal within 48 to 72 hours following specimen processing. Specialized pathology or diagnostic imaging reports require clinical review and are released within 5 business days alongside notes from your physician.
